# Sign in with the Apple ID you have

> **Preview** · 2026-10-17 · `0.1.1.5950`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

No new password to remember on iOS: sign in with Apple.

Choose Sign in with Apple on the iOS sign-in screen, and your Apple ID opens your Mars account. No separate password to set up.

One tap into Mars, with an Apple ID you already have.
