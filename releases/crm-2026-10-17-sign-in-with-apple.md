# Sign in with Apple

> **Preview** · 2026-10-17 · `0.1.1.5950`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Sign in to Mars on iOS with the Apple ID you already use.

Choose Sign in with Apple on the iOS sign-in screen. Your Apple ID then opens your Mars account.

One tap to your Mars account, using an Apple ID you already have.
