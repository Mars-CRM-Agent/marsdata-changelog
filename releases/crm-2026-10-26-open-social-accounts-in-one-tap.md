# Open social accounts in one tap

> **Preview** · 2026-10-26 · `0.1.1.8385`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

When you ask for someone's accounts, every account in the answer is ready to use.

Ask something like “What are Zhang Yuan's social accounts?” Mars answers with the accounts saved on that person's record, and each account appears as a capsule with its platform's mark. Tap it to copy the account and open the app: WeChat, Xiaohongshu, Douyin and similar apps open at their home, with the account ready to paste into search; WhatsApp and Telegram open a chat with that person; X, Instagram, LinkedIn, LINE and GitHub open their profile. An account saved as a web address, such as github.com/liming, opens at that address. Mars recognises 21 platforms, on the web and in the iOS app. On iPhone and iPad, a chat or profile link opens in the in-app browser when its app is not installed.

Go from asking about someone to adding them on WeChat or following them, without copying by hand.
