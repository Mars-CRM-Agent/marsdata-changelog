# Find in chat and a new answer menu

> **Preview** · 2026-10-17 · `0.1.1.5950`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Search inside the open conversation and act on any answer from one menu.

Find in chat searches the conversation you have open. The answer menu adds branching, read aloud with pause, a 15-second skip and speed control, model switching, retry and web search.

Find what you need in a long chat, and act on any answer from one menu.
