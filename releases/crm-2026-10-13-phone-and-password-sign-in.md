# Phone and password sign-in

> **Preview** · 2026-10-13 · `0.1.1.5222`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Sign in on the web with your phone number and password.

Choose phone number and password on the web sign-in page. When Mars needs a security check, the check explains why it is needed.

Signing in stays clear, even when an extra security check is required.
