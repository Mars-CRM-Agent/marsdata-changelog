# Phone sign-in, no guesswork

> **Preview** · 2026-10-13 · `0.1.1.5222`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

A surprise security check is less annoying when you know why it's there.

Choose phone number and password on the web sign-in page. When Mars needs a security check, the check explains why. No guessing what went wrong: follow along and you are in.

Signing in stays clear, even when an extra check is needed.
