# Keep track of who knows whom

> **Preview** · 2026-10-07 · `0.1.1.4285`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

When your contacts know each other, your network gets tangled fast.

Record how two of your contacts are connected: each relationship keeps its own type and summary, so the link between two people is stored on its own instead of blurring into either record.

Your network stays clear, even when friends and colleagues know each other.
