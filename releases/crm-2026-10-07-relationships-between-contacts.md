# Relationships between contacts

> **Preview** · 2026-10-07 · `0.1.1.4285`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Record how two of your contacts are connected, with a type and a summary.

Each relationship keeps its own type and summary, so the link between two people is recorded on its own.

Your network of people stays clear when friends or colleagues know each other.
