# Gmail, read-only

> **Preview** · 2026-10-13 · `0.1.1.5222`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Connect Gmail read-only, and Mars can search and read your mail in chat.

Connect your Gmail account for read-only access. In chat, Mars can search your mail and read the messages you ask about, without changing your inbox.

Answers can draw on your email without Mars being able to change it.
