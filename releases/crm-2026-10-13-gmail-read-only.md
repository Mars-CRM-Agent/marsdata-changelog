# Let Mars find the answer in your Gmail

> **Preview** · 2026-10-13 · `0.1.1.5222`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

What did that email say? Ask instead of digging through your inbox.

Connect your Gmail account with read-only access. In chat, Mars can search your mail and read the messages you ask about, without changing your inbox. No digging on your part, and no worrying about your mail being touched.

Answers can draw on your email, while your inbox stays exactly as it was.
