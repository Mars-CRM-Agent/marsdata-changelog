# See the whole month beside the day

> **Preview** · 2026-09-23 · `0.1.1.3475`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

An empty day shouldn't hide the rest of your month.

Beside the day view, a This month list shows the month's events grouped by date; tap a date to jump to it. Days that hold events carry a dot in the mini calendar. No clicking day by day to see where things are.

See where your month is busy before you pick a day.
