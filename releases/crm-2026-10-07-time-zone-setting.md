# Times that match wherever you are

> **Preview** · 2026-10-07 · `0.1.1.4285`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Changed time zones? Your reminders should change with you.

Follow your device time zone, or pick a city in Settings. Conversations, reminders, Events and usage records all show times in the zone you choose. No time-difference maths.

Dates and times match where you are, so reminders arrive at the hour you expect.
