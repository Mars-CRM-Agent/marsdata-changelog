# Time zone setting

> **Preview** · 2026-10-07 · `0.1.1.4285`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Show chats, reminders, Events and usage in your own time zone.

Follow your device time zone, or pick a city in Settings. Conversations, reminders, Events and usage records all show times in the zone you choose.

Dates and times match where you are, so reminders arrive at the hour you expect.
