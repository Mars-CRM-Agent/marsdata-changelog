# Sort by value

> **Preview** · 2026-10-23 · `0.1.1.7618`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

See what matters first, not just what happened last.

Open the Events menu and pick Sort by value, just below Time order. The list becomes High-value events: signed contracts, funding talks, referrals and transfers rank above routine logistics, and repeated records with the same people within three days fold under one row with +N similar, which you can expand. Swipe or hold a row to complete, edit or delete it, as in the time list. On the web the desktop event-type menu offers the same order. In Relationships, Sort by value ranks contacts by their value and how often you interacted in the last half year, and each row shows the strongest reason together with the half-year interaction count. Time order stays the default, and picking a time order or another view takes you back.

Find the deals, introductions and people worth your time without scrolling through the calendar.
