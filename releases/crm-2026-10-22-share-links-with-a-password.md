# Share links with a password

> **Preview** · 2026-10-22 · `0.1.1.7433`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Decide who can read a conversation you share.

Open a conversation's share sheet and turn on Access password. Mars creates a random six-character password, or tap the boxes to type your own. Anyone who opens the link enters the password before the conversation appears, so send the password separately from the link. After ten wrong attempts the link pauses for a while. Once a conversation is shared, its menu shows View share, where you can read or change the password or turn it off at any time.

Share a conversation without worrying about who else ends up reading it.
