# One request, a deck ready to present

> **Preview** · 2026-10-24 · `0.1.1.7959`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Layouts and picture hunting can eat a whole afternoon. Hand the deck to Mars.

Ask Mars for a deck, such as a launch keynote, a weekly team update or an investor introduction, and it writes an editable PowerPoint file in the Mars style, light or dark. Each slide gets its own layout, the pictures are drawn in one consistent style without lettering, key moments become a large headline over a full-bleed picture, and speaker notes, tables or charts appear where they help; it never invents figures you did not give it. No one-size template, no hunting for pictures. Ask for changes and Mars saves a revised copy: slides you leave alone stay as they were, and only the pictures you ask to change are redrawn. Ask for a Word document, such as a getting-started guide, and it arrives in the same style with lists and illustrations. Both land in the Library, where you can page through and download them.

One request gets you a deck or document you can edit and present straight away.
