# Mars makes slide decks and documents

> **Preview** · 2026-10-24 · `0.1.1.7959`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Describe the deck you need and Mars builds it, pictures included.

Ask Mars for a deck, such as a launch keynote, a weekly team update or an investor introduction, and it writes an editable PowerPoint file in the Mars style, light or dark. It picks a layout for each slide, draws the pictures in one consistent style without lettering, sets key moments as a large headline over a full-bleed picture, and adds speaker notes and tables or charts where they help. It does not invent figures you did not give it. Ask for changes and Mars saves a revised copy: slides you leave alone stay as they were, and only the pictures you ask to change are redrawn. Ask for a Word document, such as a getting-started guide, and it arrives in the same style with lists and illustrations. Both are saved to the Library, where you can page through every slide and download the file.

A presentation or document you can open, edit and present, made from one request.
