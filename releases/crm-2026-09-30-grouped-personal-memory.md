# What Mars knows, one card per topic

> **Preview** · 2026-09-30 · `0.1.1.3581`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

The same details scattered in several places, padded with empty rows? Not anymore.

Personal Memory now groups what Mars knows about you under one heading per topic: basic details, contact info and learned facts share one card when they carry the same name. Empty fields no longer take up a row, and they come back while you edit. No flipping around, no gaps in the way.

One place per topic, with nothing empty in the way.
