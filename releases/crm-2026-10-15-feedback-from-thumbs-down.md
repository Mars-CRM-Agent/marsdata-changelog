# Feedback from thumbs-down

> **Preview** · 2026-10-15 · `0.1.1.5585`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Tell us what went wrong when an answer misses the mark.

Tap thumbs-down on an answer to open a feedback sheet. Add your report and send it to support with the conversation attached.

Support gets the full context, so problems are easier to fix.
