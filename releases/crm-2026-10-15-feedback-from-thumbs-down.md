# One thumbs-down, straight to support

> **Preview** · 2026-10-15 · `0.1.1.5585`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

A bad answer shouldn't take screenshots and an essay to report.

Tap thumbs-down on an answer to open a feedback sheet. Add your note and send it to support with the conversation attached. No screenshots, no retelling the whole story.

Support gets the full context, so problems get fixed faster.
