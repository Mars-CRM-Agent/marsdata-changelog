# Your keyboard knows the way around Mars

> **Preview** · 2026-11-02 · `0.1.1.9961`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Every trip to the mouse breaks your train of thought.

Mars now has one set of keyboard shortcuts on the web, the Mac, the iPad and an iPhone with a keyboard. ⌘K searches your conversations, people and files, ⌘J starts a new conversation, ⌘D starts voice input and ⌘B shows or hides the sidebar. ⌃⌘R, O, M, E and A take you straight to Relationships, Files, Memory, Events and Reminders. Hover a sidebar button and its tip shows the keys, so there is nothing to memorise up front. In a browser on Windows or Linux, use Ctrl for ⌘ and Ctrl + Alt for ⌃⌘. Learn them once and they work the same on every device.

Move through Mars as fast as you type.
