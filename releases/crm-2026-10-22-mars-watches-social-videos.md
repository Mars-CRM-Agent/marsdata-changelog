# Mars watches the videos for you

> **Preview** · 2026-10-22 · `0.1.1.7385`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Hear what a person says in their own videos, without watching them yourself.

When you ask Mars to find out about someone, it now watches the one or two of their Douyin or Xiaohongshu videos that people engage with most before writing the profile. You can also send a video link and ask what it says. The answer summarizes the video and quotes the speaker's own words and on-screen text, marked with the minute and second they appear. Mars names a person only from what the video shows or says, not from the account that posted it. Each relevant video is saved to your Library and linked in the answer, and the thinking panel lists the posts, accounts and videos Mars looked at. Videos up to 10 minutes and 30 MB are supported.

Know what someone actually said on camera, with the video kept in your files.
