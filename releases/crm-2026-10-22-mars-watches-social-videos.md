# Mars understands social media videos

> **Preview** · 2026-10-22 · `0.1.1.7433`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

More and more people show who they are on video. Mars now watches it for you.

Ask Mars about a person in any conversation, for example “Tell me everything about this founder.” Mars reads their own accounts on Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Weibo or Bilibili, pinned posts first, then the newest and the most liked, so the answer starts from what they post now. It then watches the videos that matter most and understands what is said and shown: it summarizes each video, quotes the speaker’s own words and on-screen text with the minute and second they appear, and names a person only from what the video shows or says, never from the account that posted it. You can also send a single video link from Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, Kuaishou or Weibo and ask what it says. Relevant videos, except Bilibili ones, are saved to your Library and linked in the answer, and the thinking panel lists every post, account and video Mars looked at. Videos up to 10 minutes and 30 MB are supported.

Know what someone actually says on camera before you meet them.
