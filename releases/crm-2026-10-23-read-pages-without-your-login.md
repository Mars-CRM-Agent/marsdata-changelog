# Web pages read without your login

> **Preview** · 2026-10-23 · `0.1.1.7576`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Your accounts stay in your hands. Mars only looks at what is public.

Send Mars a link, or let it search, and it fetches each page once and reads what a signed-out visitor sees. The request carries no account, cookie, password or saved login, and nothing stays signed in afterwards. For privacy and security, Mars does not take cookies or passwords for sites such as X (Twitter) or Guancha and does not operate a browser for you, so your own timeline, follows, messages and member-only pages stay out of its reach, while public pages and public posts stay readable. Ask Mars why it cannot log in and it answers from its Mars Wiki guide. For content that needs your login, open the original link: on the web it opens in a new tab, in the iOS app in the in-app browser or Safari, where you sign in and stay signed in.

Get the public facts from Mars without handing any account to it.
