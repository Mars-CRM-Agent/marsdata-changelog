# Add your thought mid-answer

> **Preview** · 2026-10-25 · `0.1.1.8208`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Thought of something mid-answer? Say it now.

While Mars is answering, type in the composer as usual: the send button stays available for a text-only draft. Send it and Mars finishes the part it is writing, then continues below your message with the addition taken into account, for example narrowing a list of contacts to investors only. If the answer ends first, your message goes out as the next one. No stopping, no asking the whole question again: change direction the moment you want to. A draft with files, a composer mode or people waits until the answer ends, and with an empty draft the button still stops the answer. This works on the web and in the iOS app.

Steer the answer the moment you think of something, with no waiting and no starting over.
