# Diagrams: 86% lighter, near-instant

> **Preview** · 2026-10-20 · `0.1.1.6573`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

No more waiting on a diagram, or squinting at one shrunk to fit.

Drawing diagrams now needs about 230 KB instead of about 1.6 MB, roughly 86% less, and that download only happens when an answer first shows a diagram. Each diagram then draws in 1 to 8 milliseconds. In a narrow conversation a diagram re-flows from top to bottom instead of shrinking out of legibility, on the web and on iOS, in the same outline style.

Answers with diagrams appear sooner and stay readable on any screen.
