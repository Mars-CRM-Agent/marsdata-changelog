# Faster diagrams that fit any width

> **Preview** · 2026-10-20 · `0.1.1.6573`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Diagrams in answers open faster and fit wherever you read them.

Drawing diagrams now needs a download of about 230 KB instead of about 1.6 MB, roughly 86% less, and it only happens when an answer first shows a diagram. Each diagram then draws in 1 to 8 milliseconds. In a narrow conversation a diagram re-flows from top to bottom instead of shrinking, on the web and on iOS, in the same outline style.

Answers with diagrams appear sooner and stay readable on any screen.
