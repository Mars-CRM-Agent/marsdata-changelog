# Dates that read the way you do

> **Preview** · 2026-10-10 · `0.1.1.4799`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Where the week starts and how a date is written depends on where you're from.

Set the day your week starts on and the date format you prefer, and events on the web and iOS follow your choice. Set it once, see it everywhere, with no mental conversion.

Calendars and event lists read the way you expect.
