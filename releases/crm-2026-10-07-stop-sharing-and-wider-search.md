# Revoke a link, find any old answer

> **Preview** · 2026-10-07 · `0.1.1.4285`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

A shared link you can't revoke, an old answer you can't find: both fixed.

Share links now have a Stop sharing action that turns the link off. Workspace search also covers the text inside your conversations, so a phrase you remember is enough to find an old answer. You stay in control of what you share, and finding things no longer depends on titles alone.

Close a shared link whenever you like, and find an old answer by its words.
