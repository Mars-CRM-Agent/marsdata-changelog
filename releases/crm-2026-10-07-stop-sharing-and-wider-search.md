# Stop sharing and wider search

> **Preview** · 2026-10-07 · `0.1.1.4285`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Revoke a shared link at any time, and search the text of your conversations.

Share links now have a Stop sharing action that turns the link off. Workspace search also covers the text inside your conversations.

You can close access to a shared link and find an old answer by its words.
