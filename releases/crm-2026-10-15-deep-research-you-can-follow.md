# Deep research you can watch

> **Preview** · 2026-10-15 · `0.1.1.5585`
> These features are available in the preview version and have not been generally released.

Long research shouldn't be a black box.

Each research helper appears as a card with its steps updating live, and its proposal arrives as a question card for you to confirm. No black box: you steer the direction from the start.

Follow the research as it happens and sign off on where it goes.
